...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

Moderate to fresh winds prevail over the Gulf of California, with
slight seas. Moderate or weaker winds, and moderate seas, prevail
over the open waters off Mexico. 

For the forecast, the pressure gradient between high pressure NW
of the area and low pressure over the Colorado River Valley will
support moderate to fresh winds over the northern Gulf of 
California through tonight. Elsewhere, a moderate pressure 
gradient will support mostly gentle to moderate winds across the 
offshore waters of Mexico. Fresh to strong gap winds and building
seas are expected across the Gulf of Tehuantepec toward the end 
of the week.

For the forecast, moderate to fresh winds will pulse across the
