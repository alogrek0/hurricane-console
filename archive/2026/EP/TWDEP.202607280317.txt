Tropical Weather Discussion for the eastern Pacific Ocean from
03.4S to 30N, east of 120W including the Gulf of California, and
from the Equator to 30N, between 120W and 140W. The following
information is based on satellite imagery, weather observations,
radar, and meteorological analysis.

Based on 0000 UTC surface analysis and satellite imagery through
0230 UTC.
 
...SPECIAL FEATURES...

Hurricane Genevieve is centered near 17.1N 114.7W at 28/0300
UTC, moving northwest at 10 kt. Estimated minimum central
pressure is 939 mb. Maximum sustained wind speed is 125 kt with
gusts to 150 kt. Peak seas are near 43 ft or 13 m. Numerous 
strong convection is noted within 90 nm of the center, and
numerous moderate convection is within 180 nm of the center. 
A west- northwestward to northwestward motion with some decrease
in forward speed is expected during the next couple of days. 
Genevieve is a category 4 hurricane on the Saffir-Simpson 
Hurricane Wind Scale. Weakening is forecast this week. Swells
generated by Genevieve area affecting the west coast of the Baja
California Peninsula and portions of the southwestern coast of 
Mexico. These swells are likely to cause life-threatening surf 
and rip current conditions. Please consult products from your 
local weather office. Please read the latest HIGH SEAS FORECAST 
issued by the National Hurricane Center at website - 
https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/text/MIAHSFEP2.shtml and the latest 
Genevieve NHC Forecast/Advisory and Public Advisory at 
www.hurricanes.gov for more details.

...TROPICAL WAVES...

A tropical wave is along 101W, south of 15N just off the coast 
of Guerrero, moving westward at around 15 kt. Scattered moderate
convection is observed from 04N to 15N between 99W and 103W. 

...INTERTROPICAL CONVERGENCE ZONE/MONSOON TROUGH...

The monsoon trough extends from 1010 mb low pressure near the
Panama-Colombia border around 09N77W to 08N87W to 07N98W to
11N108W. The ITCZ extends from 07N128W to beyond 05N140W. Other 
than the convection mentioned with Genevieve and the tropical 
wave above, numerous moderate convection is noted from 01N to 
09N between 77W and 92W, from 00N to 08N between 128W and 140W,
and within 180 nm of the coast of Mexico between 103W and 108W.

Away form the impacts of Genevieve, moderate to locally fresh NW
winds associated with high pressure building in from the
northwest are impacting waters N and W of Guadalupe Island.

winds prevail. Seas are slight in the Gulf of California, and 
moderate in the remaining offshore waters.

night nearshore Baja California and also in the Gulf of 
California as high pressure builds southward to the west of the
