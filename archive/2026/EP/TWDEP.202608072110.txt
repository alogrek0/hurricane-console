The monsoon trough extends from 09N78W to 10N92W. Segments of 
the ITCZ extend from 09N95W to 10N108W, from 10N113W to 11N128W 
and from 10N132W to 11N140W. Scattered moderate convection is

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

Fresh to strong winds and moderate to locally rough seas are 
over the Gulf of Tehuantepec. Moderate or weaker winds, and 
moderate seas, prevail over the remainder of the open waters off 
Mexico. Gentle winds and slight seas are over the Gulf of 
California.

For the forecast, fresh to strong gap winds and rough seas are 
expected across the Gulf of Tehuantepec into the weekend. 
Elsewhere, a moderate pressure gradient will support mostly 
gentle to moderate winds across the offshore waters of Mexico. 
The pressure gradient between high pressure NW of the area and 
lower pressure over the Colorado River Valley will support fresh 
southerly winds across the northern Gulf of California late this 
weekend into early next week. 

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

A recent scatterometer satellite pass confirmed fresh to strong 
winds across and downstream of the Gulf of Papagayo. Rough seas
likely accompany the plume of gap winds. Light to gentle winds 
and moderate seas are noted elsewhere.

For the forecast, fresh to strong gap winds and occasional rough
seas will pulse across the Gulf of Papagayo into early next 
week. Moderate or weaker winds and moderate seas in SW swell are 
expected across the remainder of the waters through the period, 
except for large SE to S swell near the Galapagos early next 
week. 

...REMAINDER OF THE AREA...

High pressure prevails over the waters N of 20N. The pressure
gradient between this area of high pressure and a tropical wave
just west of 140W is supporting moderate to fresh winds from 15N
to 20N and W of 135W. Seas over these waters are near 8 ft. 
Rough seas to 8 ft are found over the waters S of 05N between 
100W and 120W. Elsewhere, gentle to moderate winds and moderate 
seas prevail.

For the forecast, seas to 8 ft in the western tradewind waters 
will subside by Sat. Farther south, southerly swell to 8 ft will
impact the waters south of 05N and west of 100W through the 
weekend.
