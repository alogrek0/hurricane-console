...SPECIAL FEATURES...

Hurricane Fausto is centered near 18.7N 133.9W, or about 500 nm 
east of Hilo, Hawaii at 25/0300 UTC, moving west at 12 kt. 
Estimated minimum central pressure is 967 mb. Maximum sustained 
wind speed is 90 kt with gusts to 110 kt. Latest satellite
imagery shows that Fausto's overall structure has changed little
during the past several hours. The hurricane continues to 
exhibit a well-defined and fairly symmetric inner core. The
imagery reveals numerous moderate to strong convection within 
180 nm of the center in the NE and SW quadrants, within 150 nm 
in the SE quadrant and 120 nm in the NW quadrant. Fausto is 
forecast to maintain its current motion for the next several 
days. Gradual weakening is forecast during the next several days.
Swells generated by Fausto are affecting the west coast of the 
Baja California Peninsula and southern California, and will begin
to affect the Hawaiian Islands by Sun. These swells are likely 
to cause life-threatening surf and rip current conditions. Please
consult products from your local weather office.

Tropical Storm Genevieve is centered near 9.5N 102.6W at 25/0300
UTC, moving west-northwest at 10 kt. Estimated minimum central
pressure is 1000 mb. Maximum sustained wind speed is 45 kt with
gusts to 55 kt. Satellite imagery shows that numerous strong
convection is increasing over the center of Genevieve. The 
imagery depicts forming banding features mainly in the NW 
semicircle of Genevieve. Convection with these banding features 
is of the numerous moderate to strong type intensity within 60 nm
either side of a line from 13N101W to 11N104W to 08N105W and to 
8.5N103W. Scattered moderate to isolated strong convection is 
within 30 nm of a line from 07N103W to 05N106W. Genevieve is 
forecast to stay well offshore of the Mexican coast during the 
next few days as it continues in west-northwesward motion. 
Significant to rapid intensification is expected during the next 
few days. Genevieve is forecast to become a hurricane on Sat and 
a major hurricane by late Sun. Swells generated by Genevieve are 
expected to begin affecting portions of the southwestern coast 
Mexico over the weekend and will likely reach southern portions 
of the Baja California peninsula on Mon. These swells are likely 
to cause life-threatening surf and rip current conditions. Please
consult products from your local weather office.

...TROPICAL WAVES...

The southern extension of the tropical wave extends along 86W 
north of 05N to inland central Honduras and western Nicaragua.
It is moving westward at 10 to 15 kt. Scattered strong
convection is within 60 nm east of the wave from 10N to 11N.
Isolated showers are elsewhere near the wave axis.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

A ridge extends northwest to southwest over the waters west of
Baja California. The gradient between the ridge and relatively
lower pressures over Baja California is supporting moderate to 
fresh northwest to north winds over the waters west of Baja 
California north of Cabo San Lazaro and gentle to moderate 
northwest winds south of Cabo San Lazaro. Seas over these waters
are 5 to 7 ft in northwest swell. Light and variable winds are 
over waters southeast of Baja California to near 16N along with 
seas of 3 to 5 ft in south to southwest swell. Fresh to strong 
east to southeast winds in the northern outer periphery of 
Genevieve are present south of 16N along with seas 7 to 11 ft in
southeast to south swell.

For the forecast, Tropical Storm Genevieve will move to near 
near 10.4N 104.1W Sat morning, then strengthen to a hurricane 
near 11.6N 106.5W Sat evening with maximum sustained winds 70 kt 
gusts 85 kt, to near near 12.8N 108.8W Sun morning, and begin to 
rapidly intensify as it reaches to near 14.0N 110.8W Sun evening,
then to near 15.4N 112.6W Mon morning as a major hurricane with 
maximum sustained 110 kt gusts 135 kt and move to near 16.6N 
114.1W Mon evening. Genevieve will change little in intensity as 
it moves to near 18.1N 117.0W Tue evening. As Genevieve tracks to
the southwest and west of Baja california Sur next week, the 
pressure gradient between it and high pressure to its north the

Swells from Genevieve will begin to impact the offshore zones 
from south to north beginning at building seas over these waters

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

Fresh north to northeast winds continue across the Papagayo 
region and extending downwind to near 91W, and northward to 
northwestern Nicaragua. Seas are 5 to 7 ft with these winds. The
monsoon trough remains active across the offshore waters. 
Elsewhere, winds are moderate or weaker with seas 4 to 5 ft in S 
to SW swell.

For the forecast, fresh to strong northeast to east winds and 
moderate to rough seas will pulse across the Papagayo region 
well into next week. Gentle to moderate north winds are expected
in the Gulf of Panama into early next week with slight to 
moderate seas. Moderate or weaker winds and moderate seas in 
southwest swell are expected elsewhere, except in the immediate 
lee of the Galapagos Islands and nearshore western Colombia where
slight seas are forecast.
