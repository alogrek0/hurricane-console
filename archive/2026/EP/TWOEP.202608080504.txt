Western East Pacific and Central Pacific:
A tropical wave located well east-southeast of the Hawaiian Islands

South of Baja California:

* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.

Southwest of Mexico:

development of this system is possible thereafter as it moves 
generally west-northwestward.
* Formation chance through 48 hours...low...near 0 percent.
* Formation chance through 7 days...low...20 percent.
