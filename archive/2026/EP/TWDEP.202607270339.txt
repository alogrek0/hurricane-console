Tropical Weather Discussion for the eastern Pacific Ocean from
03.4S to 30N, east of 120W including the Gulf of California, and

Based on 0000 UTC surface analysis and satellite imagery through

...SPECIAL FEATURES...

Hurricane Genevieve is centered near 14.7N 111.2W at 27/0300
UTC, moving west-northwest at 12 kt. Estimated minimum central
pressure is 929 mb. Maximum sustained wind speed is 135 kt with
gusts to 165 kt. Peak seas are near 36 ft (11 M). Numerous strong
convection is noted within 90 nm of the center. A west- 
northwestward to northwestward motion with some decrease in 
forward speed is expected during the next few days. Genevieve is 
a Category 4 hurricane on the Saffir-Simpson Hurricane Wind 
Scale. Some additional strengthening is possible during the next 
day or so. Genevieve is then forecast to start weakening by the 
middle of this week. Swells generated by Genevieve are affecting 
portions of the southwestern coast of Mexico and will likely 
reach southern portions of the Baja California Peninsula tonight 
or Monday. These swells are likely to cause life-threatening surf
and rip current conditions. Please consult products from your 
local weather office.

Please read the latest HIGH SEAS FORECAST issued by the National
Hurricane Center at website - 
https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/text/MIAHSFEP2.shtml and the latest NHC 
Forecast/Advisories and Public Advisories of both systems at 
www.hurricanes.gov for more details.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

Please refer to the Special Features section for information on 
Hurricane Genevieve, currently located about 500 nm S of Baja

Away form the impacts of Genevieve, gentle winds dominate most of

of California.

High pressure north of the area is forcing fresh  easterly trade
winds and seas of 5 to 7 ft across the Gulf of Papagayo region. 
Elsewhere, moderate or lighter winds and moderate seas are

For the forecast, fresh to strong northeast to east winds will 
pulse across the Papagayo region through the period. Moderate to
