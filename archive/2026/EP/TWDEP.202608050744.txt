...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

Moderate to fresh winds prevail over the Gulf of California, with
slight seas. Moderate or weaker winds, and moderate seas, prevail
over the open waters off Mexico. 

For the forecast, the pressure gradient between high pressure NW of
the area and low pressure over the Colorado River valley will
support moderate to fresh winds over the northern Gulf of
California today. Elsewhere, a moderate pressure gradient will 
support mostly gentle to moderate winds across the offshore 
waters of Mexico. Fresh to strong gap winds and building seas are
expected across the Gulf of Tehuantepec toward the end of the 
week. 

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

Moderate to fresh winds and moderate seas prevail over and 
downstream the Gulf of Papagayo. Gentle to moderate winds are 
found between the Galapagos Islands and Ecuador. Light to gentle 
winds and moderate seas are noted elsewhere. 

For the forecast, moderate to fresh winds will pulse across the
Papagayo region, with strong winds and occasional rough seas at 
night the second half of the week. Moderate or weaker winds and 
moderate seas in SW swell are expected across the remainder of 
the waters through the period. 

...REMAINDER OF THE AREA...

Gentle to moderate winds and moderate seas prevail across the
discussion waters.

For the forecast, the rough seas in northerly swell will subside
this evening. Southerly swell will build northward to 03N and 
west of 105W starting tonight and Wed. Seas may build to 8 ft in
