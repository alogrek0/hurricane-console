Tropical Weather Discussion
NWS National Hurricane Center Miami FL
1605 UTC Fri Jul 24 2026

Tropical Weather Discussion for the eastern Pacific Ocean from
03.4S to 30N, east of 120W including the Gulf of California, and
from the Equator to 30N, between 120W and 140W. The following
information is based on satellite imagery, weather observations,
radar, and meteorological analysis.

Based on 1200 UTC surface analysis and satellite imagery through
1530 UTC.
 
...SPECIAL FEATURES...

Hurricane Fausto is centered near 18.6N 131.2W at 24/1500 UTC,
moving west at 14 kt. Estimated minimum central pressure is 967
mb. Maximum sustained wind speed is 90 kt with gusts to 110 kt.
Numerous moderate to strong convection is from 17N to 21N between
128W and 133W. Seas peaking to 28 ft are expected in the area of
strongest winds, and 12 ft seas or greater within 180 nm S 
semicircle, 270 nm NE quadrant and 240 nm NW quadrant. Swells 
generated by Fausto are affecting the west coast of the Baja 
California Peninsula and the coast of southern California. These 
swells are likely to cause life- threatening surf and rip current
conditions. Please consult products from your local weather 
office. Fausto is expected to gradually weaken during the next 
several days.

Tropical Depression Seven-E is centered near 9.0N 100.5W at
24/1500 UTC, moving west-northwest at 10 kt. Estimated minimum
central pressure is 1005 mb. Maximum sustained wind speed is 30
kt with gusts to 40 kt. Seas to 11 ft are noted within the area 
of strongest winds. Scattered moderate to strong convection 
prevails from 04N to 15N between 94W and 109W. Significant 
strengthening is forecast during the next few days. The system is
expected to become a tropical storm later today or tonight and 
could reach hurricane strength by late this weekend.

Please read the latest HIGH SEAS FORECAST issued by the National
Hurricane Center at website -
https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/text/MIAHSFEP2.shtml and the latest
NHC Forecast/Advisories and Public Advisories of both systems at
www.hurricanes.gov for more details.

...TROPICAL WAVES...

The southern extension of the tropical wave extends along 83W and
N of 06N, moving W at 10 to 15 kt. Scattered moderate convection
is noted in the vicinity of the wave axis, mainly E of 87W. 

...INTERTROPICAL CONVERGENCE ZONE/MONSOON TROUGH...

The monsoon trough extends from 10N86W to 07N108W. The ITCZ is
from 07N108W to 04N127W, then resumes near 08N132W to 07N140W.
Scattered moderate to strong convection is noted within 240 nm on
either side of the boundaries.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

In the wake of Fausto a ridge is building across the offshore 
waters of Baja California and to the Revillagigedo Islands. This 
pattern supports fresh to strong NW winds north of Punta Eugenia, 
and mainly gentle to moderate northwest to north winds between Punta 
Eugenia and Cabo San Lucas. Seas generating by Fausto are still 
spreading across the area, and mixing with the building northerly 
wind waves to produce seas 6 to 8 ft across the Baja waters, highest 
N of Punta Eugenia. Gentle to moderate NW to W winds and moderate 
seas prevail across the remainder of the Mexican offshore waters.

For the forecast, Tropical Depression Seven-E will strengthen to
a tropical storm near 9.3N 101.7W this evening, move to 10.5N 
103.7W Sat morning, 11.7N 106.2W Sat evening, strengthen to a 
hurricane near 12.9N 108.5W Sun morning, 14.0N 110.5W Sun 
evening, and 15.3N 112.3W Mon morning. Seven-E will change little
in intensity as it moves 17.2N 115.0W by early Tue. Otherwise, 
the pressure gradient between high pressure building in the wake 
of Fausto and a trough over the Gulf of California is producing 
fresh to strong north winds north of Punta Eugenia. These winds 
will diminish this afternoon. 

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

Fresh to strong north to northeast winds continue across the 
Papagayo region and extending downwind to near 91W, and northward
to northwestern Nicaragua. Seas are 5 to 8 ft with these winds 
based on altimeter data. This is occurring behind a tropical wave
with axis along 96W. The monsoon trough remains active across 
the offshore waters. Elsewhere, winds are moderate or weaker with
seas 4 to 5 ft in S to SW swell.

For the forecast, fresh to strong northeast to east winds will 
pulse at night and into the morning hours across the Papagayo 
region well into next week. Moderate or weaker winds along with 
moderate seas in SW swell will continue elsewhere. Seas 
associated with a developing tropical system near new may 
impact the offshore waters of Guatemala through Fri, building 
seas to around 9 ft. Cross-equatorial southerly swell will begin
to reach the Galapagos Islands by early this evening and build 
through the weekend and into Mon before decaying Tue.

Hurricane Fausto dominates conditions over the High Seas forecast
waters roughly from 12N to 27N between 121W and 135W. Fresh to

extend northward to 24N, with seas to 8 ft reaching as far N of

dominates the waters between Baja California and Fausto, and 
also north of 15N west of about 130W. Moderate or weaker winds 
with moderate seas prevail. Southwest swell will begin to build 
seas over the equatorial waters west of 120W by early this 
evening.
