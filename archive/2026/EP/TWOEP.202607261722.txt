The National Hurricane Center is issuing advisories on Hurricane

Well West-Southwest of Hawaii (CP92):
Showers and thunderstorms associated with a broad area of low 
pressure located well west-southwest of the Hawaiian Islands 
continue to show signs of organization. Environmental conditions 
appear conducive for additional development and a tropical 
depression is expected to form over the next day or so as this 
system moves generally westward at 10-15 mph. This system is 
crossing the International Date Line and entering the western 
Pacific basin. Therefore, this is the last Tropical Weather Outlook 
issued by the National Hurricane Center on this system. Future 
information of this system can be found in products issued by the 
Joint Typhoon Warning Center and the Japan Meteorological Agency.
