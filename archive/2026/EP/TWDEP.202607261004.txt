Tropical Weather Discussion for the eastern Pacific Ocean from

...SPECIAL FEATURES...

Hurricane Fausto is centered near 19.7N 139.8W, or about 860 nm 
east of Hilo, Hawaii at 26/0900 UTC, moving west at 11 kt. 
Estimated minimum central pressure is 974 mb. Maximum sustained 
wind speed is 80 kt with gusts to 100 kt. Peak seas are around 
34 ft. Satellite imagery indicates that Fausto's overall 
structure has begun to deteriorate during the past several hours.
The imagery reveals numerous to isolated strong convection 
within 120 NM in the W semicircle of the center. Scattered 
moderate convection is within 90 nm E semicircle of the center.
Fausto will cross 140W shortly,  however, fresh to near gale- 
force winds will linger from 18N to 25N west of 136W along with 
seas of 8 to 11 ft. These conditions will subside by early Mon 
afternoon as Fausto continues to pull farther west of the 
discussion area. 

Hurricane Genevieve is centered near 12.9N 108.3W, or about 450 
nm south-southwest of of Manzanillo, Mexico at 26/0900 UTC, 
moving west-northwest at 15 kt. Estimated minimum central 
pressure is 980 mb. Maximum sustained wind speed is 80 kt with 
gusts to 100 kt. Peak seas are up to around 32 ft. Satellite 
imagery that Genevieve is quickly becoming bettor organized and 
intensifying. Well-established upper-level outflow is noted in 
nearly all of the quadrants. The imagery shows a small ragged 
eye and banding features tightly wrapping around it. Numerous 
strong convection is within 60 nm of the center in the N
semicircle, within 90 nm of the center in the SE quadrant, 
within 120 nm in the SW quadrant and 60 nm in the NW quadrant. 
Numerous moderate to isolated strong convection marks an outer 
rain band within 60 nm of line from 11N105W to 07N108W to 07N108W
to 06N114W and to 06.5N118W. Some decrease in forward speed is 
expected during the next few days. Continued rapid 
intensification is expected during the next day or so, and 
Genevieve is forecast to become a major hurricane by late today. 
Swells generated by Genevieve are affecting portions of the 
southwestern coast of Mexico and will likely reach southern 
portions of the Baja California Peninsula Sun night or on Mon. 
These swells are likely to cause life- threatening surf and rip 
current conditions. Please consult products from your local 
weather office. Genevieve is forecast to become a hurricane by 
tonight and a major hurricane by Monday.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

A surface ridge extends northwest to southwest over the waters 
west of Baja California. The gradient between the ridge and 
relatively lower pressures over Baja California is supporting 
moderate to fresh northwest winds over the waters west of Baja 
California, primarily north of Punta Eugenia, mostly gentle
northwest to north winds south of Punta Eugenia. Seas over 
the waters west of Baja California are 4 to 6 ft in northwest 
swell, except for slightly higher seas of 5 to 7 ft north of 
Punta Eugenia. Light and variable winds are over waters southeast
of Baja California to near 18N along with seas of 3 to 5 ft in 
south to southwest swell. Fresh to strong east to southeast winds
in the northern outer periphery of now Hurricane Genevieve are 
present south of 18N along with seas 7 to 11 ft in southeast to 
south swell.

For the forecast, Genevieve will rapidly intensify as it moves
near 13.8N 109.8W this afternoon with maximum sustained winds 
100 kt gusts 120 kt, to near 14.9N 111.8W late tonight, and 
continue to rapidly intensify as it moves to near 16.1N 113.4W 
Mon afternoon with maximum sustained winds 125 kt gusts 150 kt, 
move to near 17.1N 114.9W late Mon night, then begin to gradually
weaken as it moves to near 17.9N 116.3W Tue afternoon, to near 
18.5N 117.9W late Tue night with maximum sustained winds 105 kt 
gusts 130 kt and change little in intensity as it moves to near 
19.8N 121.3W late Wed night. As Genevieve tracks in a general 
west- northwestward to northwestward motion west of Baja 
California Sur next week, the pressure gradient between it and 
high pressure to its north the pressure gradient will tighten 
west of Baja California Norte. Swells from Genevieve will impact 
the waters from the state of Guerrero north to offshore Baja 
California Norte, including the southern portion of the Gulf of 
California through most of the upcoming week.
