...INTERTROPICAL CONVERGENCE ZONE/MONSOON TROUGH...

The monsoon trough extends from 07N78W to 11N110W to a 1011 mb
low pres near 13N126W to 06N136W. The ITCZ stretches from 06N136W
to beyond 05N140W. Scattered moderate to isolated strong 
convection is occurring from 02N to 18N and east of 115W.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

The pressure gradient between the subtropical ridge that extends
to the Revillagigedo Islands and lower pressures in NW Mexico
support fresh to locally strong SE winds. This was noted in a
recent scatterometer satellite pass. Seas in these waters are 2-4
ft. Elsewhere in the Mexican offshore waters, moderate or weaker
winds and moderate seas are prevalent. A few showers and 
isolated thunderstorms are noted off southern and SW Mexico. 
Gusty winds and suddenly higher seas are possible near the 
stronger storms.

For the forecast, the pressure gradient between high pressure 
NW of the area and low pressure over the Colorado River Valley 
will support moderate to locally strong winds over the northern 
Gulf of California through Tue night. Elsewhere, a moderate 
pressure gradient will support mostly gentle to moderate winds 
across the offshore waters of Mexico. Northerly swell NW of
Guadalupe Island will subside below 8 ft tonight. Meanwhile, fresh
to strong gap winds and building seas are expected across the 
Gulf of Tehuantepec toward the end of the week.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

The subtropical ridge centered north of the Caribbean continues 
to support moderate to locally fresh easterly trade winds and 
moderate seas in the Gulf of Papagayo, extending downstream to 
about 92W. A recent scatterometer satellite pass indicate that
moderate to locally fresh southerly winds are occurring south of
04N. Seas in these waters are 4-7 ft. Light to gentle winds and 
moderate seas are noted elsewhere.

For the forecast, moderate to fresh winds will pulse across the
Papagayo region, with occasionally strong winds pulsing night
starting Tue night. Moderate or weaker winds and moderate seas 
in SW swell are expected across the remainder of the waters 
through the period. 

...REMAINDER OF THE AREA...

Moderate to fresh easterly winds and seas of 6-9 ft are present
in the far northwest waters due to the remnants of Genevieve
gradually losing steam near 26N143W. In the northern waters, 
moderate northerly winds and seas of 7-9 ft are found north of 
28N and east of 132W. High pressure dominates the remainder of 
the area, sustaining moderate or weaker winds and moderate seas.

For the forecast, subsiding northerly swell will bring rough 
seas over the waters off Baja California Norte north of 27N and 
east of 135W through Tue. Southerly swell will build northward 
to 03N and west of 105W starting Tue night. Meanwhile, seas may 
build to 8 ft in the western trade waters late this week.
