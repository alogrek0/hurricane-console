...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

Moderate or weaker winds, and moderate seas, prevail over the 
open waters off Mexico. Gentle to moderate winds, and slight to
moderate seas, are over the Gulf of California.

For the forecast, fresh to strong gap winds and building seas 
are expected across the Gulf of Tehuantepec into the weekend. 
Elsewhere, a moderate pressure gradient will support mostly 
gentle to moderate winds across the offshore waters of Mexico. 
The pressure gradient between high pressure NW of the area and 
lower pressure over the Colorado River Valley will support fresh 
southerly winds across the northern Gulf of California late this 
weekend into early next week.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

Fresh to strong winds, and moderate seas, prevail over and 
downstream the Gulf of Papagayo. Gentle to moderate winds are 
found between the Galapagos Islands and Ecuador. Light to gentle 
winds and moderate seas are noted elsewhere. 

For the forecast, fresh to strong gap winds and occasional rough
seas will pulse across the Gulf of Papagayo into early next 
week. Moderate or weaker winds and moderate seas in SW swell are 
expected across the remainder of the waters through the period. 

...REMAINDER OF THE AREA...

High pressure prevails over the waters N of 20N. The pressure
gradient between this area of high pressure and a tropical wave
near 137/138W is supporting moderate to fresh winds from 15N to
20N and W of 130W. Seas over these waters are estimated to be 
approaching 8 ft in the area of fresh winds. Elsewhere, gentle to
moderate winds and moderate seas prevail across the discussion 
waters.

For the forecast, seas to 8 ft will accompany the tropical wave 
as it moves westward through Thu night. Farther south, southerly
swell to 8 ft will move into the waters south of 05N and west of
100W through the latter part of the week.
