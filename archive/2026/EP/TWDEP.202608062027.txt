...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

Fresh to strong winds and rough seas are over the Gulf of 
Tehuantepec. Moderate or weaker winds, and moderate seas, prevail
over the remainder of the open waters off Mexico. Gentle to 
locally moderate winds and slight to moderate seas are over the 
Gulf of California.

For the forecast, fresh to strong gap winds and rough seas are 
expected across the Gulf of Tehuantepec into the weekend. 
Elsewhere, a moderate pressure gradient will support mostly 
gentle to moderate winds across the offshore waters of Mexico. 
Looking ahead, the pressure gradient between high pressure NW of
the area and lower pressure over the Colorado River Valley will 
support fresh southerly winds across the northern Gulf of 
California late this weekend into early next week.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

Fresh to strong winds, and moderate seas, prevail over and 
downstream the Gulf of Papagayo. Gentle to moderate winds are 
found between the Galapagos Islands and Ecuador. Light to gentle 
winds and moderate seas are noted elsewhere. 

For the forecast, fresh to strong gap winds and occasional rough seas
will pulse across the Gulf of Papagayo into early next week.
Moderate or weaker winds and moderate seas in SW swell are
expected across the remainder of the waters through the period,
except for large SE to S swell near the Galapagos early next 
week. 

...REMAINDER OF THE AREA...

High pressure prevails over the waters N of 20N. The pressure

near 139W is supporting moderate to fresh to strong winds are
active from 15N to 20N and W of 130W. Seas over these waters are
near 8 ft in the area of fresh to strong winds. Elsewhere, 
gentle to moderate winds and moderate seas prevail across the

For the forecast, seas to 8 ft will accompany the tropical wave 
as it moves westward through tonight. Farther south, southerly 
swell to 8 ft will move into the waters south of 05N and west of
