...SPECIAL FEATURES...

Gale warning: Post-Tropical Cyclone Genevieve is centered near 24.8N 139.5W at
02/2100 UTC, moving west-northwest at 11 kt. Estimated minimum central
pressure is 1009 mb. Maximum sustained wind speed is 35 kt with 
gusts to 45 kt. Peak seas are near 16 ft or 5 m. No significant 
shower or thunderstorm activity is evident near the center of 
this low pressure system. Genevieve will become a remnant low and
move to 25.4N 141.0W Mon morning then gradually dissipate 
through mid week as it continues to move west- northwest.

Please read the latest HIGH SEAS FORECAST issued

Genevieve NHC Forecast/Advisory and Public Advisory at

...INTERTROPICAL CONVERGENCE ZONE/MONSOON TROUGH...

The monsoon trough extends from 11N85W to 08N95W to 10N120W. The
ITCZ extends from 09N125W to 07N135W to 07N140W. Scattered
moderate convection is active from 04N to 10N between 80W and
90W, and from 10N to 15N between 95W to 125W.

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF MEXICO...

Moderate to fresh winds are over the northern Gulf of 
California. Gentle to moderate winds prevail elsewhere across 
the region. Moderate seas dominate the open waters off Mexico, 
with slight seas in the Gulf of California. 

For the forecast, the pressure gradient between high pressure west of
the area and lowering pressure over the Colorado River Valley
will support fresh SW winds and building seas over the northern
Gulf of California through the early part of next week.
Elsewhere, a moderate pressure gradient will support mostly
gentle to moderate NW winds across the offshore waters of
Mexico. Long- period NW swell will move into the waters off Baja
California Norte tonight then start to subside Tue. Looking
ahead, expect fresh to strong gap winds and building seas across
the Gulf of Tehuantepec Thu and Fri. 

...OFFSHORE WATERS WITHIN 250 NM OF CENTRAL AMERICA, COLOMBIA, 
AND WITHIN 750 NM OF ECUADOR...

Fresh winds and moderate seas prevail over and downstream the 
Gulf of Papagayo. Gentle to moderate winds are found between the 
Galapagos Islands and Ecuador. Light to gentle winds and moderate
seas are noted elsewhere. 

For the forecast, moderate to fresh winds will pulse across the 
Papagayo region tonight, and again by mid week with rough seas. 
Moderate or weaker winds and moderate seas in SW swell are 
expected across the remainder of the waters through the period.
